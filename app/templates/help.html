How Ulat works

Ulat helps the City DRRM Office turn the reports people send into the forms the office files. It drafts; you decide.

Where to find things

Home
Everything that needs you, most urgent first. Each card has one button.
Review
Reports Ulat could not settle alone: a report that may repeat another, an unknown barangay, something to double-check or a fallen tree to confirm. The number on Review is how many wait.
Incidents
Reports about the same flood or damage, put together per barangay, with their photos and timeline.
Drafts
The RDANA forms, the damaged-houses table and the Notices of Loss. Nothing is sent or filed until you approve it, and the fisherfolk file their own Notice of Loss.
More
All reports, the map, the damaged-houses table and this page.

Simple and Technical view

Ulat opens in the Simple view. The Technical view adds how Ulat worked on each report: the AI models it used, what they cost, how accurate they are, and the raw tables. Switch between them from the account button at the top right. Both views have the same buttons and do the same things.
