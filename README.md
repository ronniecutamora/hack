# Ulat

Ulat turns typhoon and flood reports into draft damage assessments and insurance forms.
It is a prototype for residents, fish farmers and the city's disaster office in Biñan, Philippines.

[Live dashboard](https://ronniecutamora47--ulat.modal.run) (passcode in the private judging notes) · [Telegram bot @UlatPH_bot](https://t.me/UlatPH_bot) · [YouTube link: added at submission]

## The problem

- After Typhoon Paeng, Biñan had 2,088 affected families on November 4, 2022, 20,087 on November 18, and 575 still displaced on December 24. [See the reports.](docs/how-it-works.md#the-problem)
- The city's damage assessment form is due within [72 hours](#official-names) of the assessment team's deployment.
- Insured fish farmers have [2 calendar days](#official-names) after a loss to submit a written notice, before harvest.

## How it works

1. A resident sends the bot a photo, short video, voice note, audio file, text or location.
2. Ulat reads the report, checks photos and finds its neighborhood (barangay).
3. It groups reports about the same incident and marks uncertain cases for an officer.
4. It drafts the city's damage assessment, damaged-houses table and insurance forms from the reports.
5. An officer checks the evidence, fills missing details and approves or rejects each draft.

Rice insurance forms are an option for offices that enable them. They are off for Biñan until a local rice area is verified.

## How you can check the agent

- Open a report's [step-by-step record](app/agent_trace.py) to see its decisions, model, timing and estimated costs. Successful calls and saved answers are logged; [failed requests and retries](app/meta/base.py) may not appear separately. Older reports may not have a record.
- [Report-based form values](docs/how-it-works.md#officer-outputs) link back to their reports; [photo outlines](app/photo.py) let an officer inspect the image evidence.
- [Review](app/questions.py) asks one question at a time. Overrides and edits require initials; [approvals are logged](app/drafts.py) with initials optional. Ulat never files a form: a person files it after review.
- Check [labeled results](data/eval/results.json) and [rerun tests with saved model answers](docs/evaluation.md) when available. Run the [offline smoke test](scripts/smoke.py); [guard tests](tests/test_no_contributor_models.py) and [US English checks](tests/test_us_english.py) protect the project.

## Open weights, same agent

In a local setup, set [`AGENT_LLM`](app/config.py) to select Spark 1.3 through Meta, Spark Open on Baseten, or Spark Open on Fireworks. `PAIR_JUDGE_LLM` can select the report-matching model separately; otherwise it follows the agent. Fireworks support has mocked tests, with no live call recorded. Muse Glimmer is also implemented on Fireworks, but its live availability is unverified.

The checked-in [production configuration](app/deploy_targets.py) uses the default Spark 1.3 for both jobs. The live setting has not been independently checked for this update.

These agreement scores account for chance; higher is better. The labels and sets are small:

Meta [offers the weights for self-hosting](https://dev.meta.ai/docs/muse-spark-open); a city could host them with suitable equipment and an integration that Ulat does not yet provide. The Philippine [Data Privacy Act](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act-/) applies to personal information. Ulat's voice and image services still run separately.

## Proof

<!-- eval-headlines:start -->
- **0.60 agreement score** for roof damage on 25 real typhoon photos (Spark 1.3).
- **1.00 matching score** on 64 synthetic report pairs (Spark 1.3; a small, easy set).
- **0 of 30** synthetic disaster reports wrongly filtered among 60 test messages.
- **19.1% word error rate** on 8 voice notes in Tagalog and English, read by one speaker (the bot's setting).
<!-- eval-headlines:end -->

The [full evaluation](docs/evaluation.md) explains the scores, test sets and limits. These are small tests, not measurements of citywide performance.
